The story behind RogueBorn

ABOUT ROGUEBORN

A student-created destination for discovering games, exploring genres, and finding something new to play.

Our Mission

RogueBorn helps players discover games across three genres: Roguelike & Roguelite, Competitive & Battle Royale, and Cyberpunk & Sci-Fi. Our goal is to make exploring new games simple, interesting, and enjoyable.

About the Creator

RogueBorn was created as a student web design project by someone interested in video games and technology. The idea was to build a useful, easy-to-explore website that connects players with games they may enjoy.

Why Are We Here?

With so many games available, choosing what to play can be difficult. RogueBorn brings together 24 games in three organized categories, with cover art, search and filters, and links to official game websites. As the project grows, we hope to improve how players discover new experiences.

What You Can Do

Browse games by genre, search for a specific title, view its cover art, and follow a link to learn more from its official website. You can also visit our Contact Us page to suggest a game or share feedback.
